**2. Vincent**
- **Cape is in the file.** It's a fifth mesh block at `0x19f70` (named `p000_L02`, 112 triangles). Its UVs match only the dump's cape texture `0x2e68` and nothing else. The dump's 224 = 2×112, possibly drawn two-sided (my guess).
- **Why it's dropped:** that block has 0 skinning batches (`cnt=0`). So `build_group_pool` returns None (`render_skinned.py:167`) and `decode_groups` skips it without a warning (`render_p005.py:107-109`).
- **Where the claim came from:** `notes/session-2026-10-17-level-remap.md:34-38` guessed "separate MOD/cloth mesh" from the container listing, never tested. The shared README turned that into "procedural cloth object", and HANDOFF Open #1 copied it. No commit mentions a cape.
- **What is supported:** 0 skinning batches plus the cloth/verlet function noted in `autonomy-journal.md:298` suggest the vertex positions are cloth-driven at runtime. The cape's triangles and UVs are still in the file, not generated.
- **Why the body looks untextured:** only 658 of 1699 mesh triangles exactly match a dump draw. The other 61% get their group's majority palette (`export_p000_gpu2.py:44-48`), but groups mix palettes (e.g. group L01.g1: 70 grey vs 28 red). So red mantle and grey body get smeared.
- **Two smaller contributors:** vertex colour is clamped at 1.0 (`export_p000_gpu2.py:77`), while the PS2's colour blend allows about 2×. And the game's extra glow pass (`0x2ee8`, 596 triangles over the arm and boots) isn't reproduced.

**3. Room**
- 47.9% of triangles (85,086 of 177,476) are textured; the rest are the honest grey group `g42`, which includes the flat light-grey facet.
- I couldn't pin a wrong texture to a specific surface cheaply: my screen-point probe mostly hits the game's full-screen post-process passes.
- Two binding steps assign textures without UV checks, so they are where wrong ones would come from:
  - the deck fill gives every roughly horizontal surface inside a box the plank texture (`recover_z128_deck.py:56-80`);
  - the "seed+grow" step spreads bindings to neighbours.
- The lower-right plank-textured facets are the likely victims, but that's unconfirmed.
- The missing sky and the big wall are probably missing layers or geometry rather than wrong textures. The cause is unconfirmed.
